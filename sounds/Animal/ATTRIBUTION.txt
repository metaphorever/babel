Animal sound collection
=======================
Attribution rider - written 2026-11-25 from the readme files, embedded version/credit
resources and download notes that shipped with the original archives.
Converted to WAV from the original classic Mac sound files; audio otherwise unaltered.

Credit:   UNKNOWN. The Animal-Sounds.sit archive contained no readme, author name or
          copyright notice.

What the files themselves reveal (editing tools only, not authors):
  - Many AIFFs were saved with SoundEdit 16 v2 (Macromedia).
  - American Robin, Mourning Dove, Large monkey growing: Sound Designer II files.
  - Common Loon: "Converted by SndConverter Pro 2.0" (D. Lambert's conversion utility).
  - toucan singing: saved with "SoundEffects" (Alberto Ricci's editor).
These look like 1990s clips gathered from multimedia/sound-effect CD-ROMs, possibly
professional nature-recording libraries, but there is no way to confirm the source.
